## A considered material partner

Since 1999, Anatolia Hides has connected Istanbul leather craft with product teams and makers across Europe. We work with qualified B2B enquiries for footwear, leather goods, furniture and interior applications.

There are no prices or online orders in this catalogue. Every programme starts with a material brief, a practical sample conversation and a clear next step.

## Material library

| Material direction | Character | Typical applications |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full grain | Natural depth, visible grain, considered finishing | Footwear, bags, premium goods |
| Top grain | Refined consistency and controlled surface | Footwear, furniture, leather goods |
| Suede | Soft nap, colour depth and quiet tactility | Footwear, apparel, accessories |
| Split leather | Versatile base for selected finish directions | Interiors, goods, development programmes |

## Development brief

A useful enquiry can include:

- Leather type and preferred thickness
- Target delivery country
- Application and programme size
- Colour or finish direction
- Required sample timing
- Company and contact details
